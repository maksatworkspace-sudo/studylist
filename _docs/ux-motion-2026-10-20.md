## Motion Direction

Preserve original bitmap branding, TENEASY-inspired compact workspace, readable
status colors and existing Lucide controls. No wholesale redesign or decorative
infinite motion. Use shared 250-450ms timings for visible entry/exit, restrained
short translation/scale and opacity, plus immediate hover/press/selected feedback.
Cover auth result feedback, dialogs, toasts, page/filter/feed changes, bookmark,
support and rating actions, and meaningful loading/skeleton states.

Welcome/farewell occurs only after successful auth, never while a request is
merely pending. Errors preserve fields and focus. Prevent duplicate in-flight
auth and conflicting mutations, reject stale responses, and never flash previous
account data. Motion must not reset drafts, lose data or become a security gate.

Dialog/toast exit is controlled, including Escape/backdrop/replacement. Outgoing
content must not remain keyboard-focusable; maintain focus containment/restoration
and once-only accessible announcements. Reduced motion removes large movements,
stagger and nonessential animation while retaining prompt static feedback.

## QA Handoff And Boundaries

QA adds failing guest regressions before implementation. Cover mock, memory,
SQLite and integrated HTTP/browser behavior, wrong/expired code, recovery,
creation browser, guest -> A -> B -> logout, signed-in unlock excluded from mine,
registered anonymous ownership, direct API attempts and staged attachments.

Count checks include populated public feed with empty saved, no-match and mixed
filters, last-bookmark removal, viewer changes and late-response races. Motion
checks include normal/reduced motion, keyboard dialogs, auth failure/success and
rapid actions, slow loading and mutation rejection. Verify 1280x720, 390x844 and
320x780 with screenshots and automated final-state assertions where feasible.
